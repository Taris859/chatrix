Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What is Chatrix?
▾
Chatrix is an AI companion platform where every AI character has its own personality, memory system, communication style, and emotional intelligence. Unlike traditional chatbots, Chatrix companions are designed for long-term, meaningful relationships — not just one-off task completion. Think of them less as tools and more as people you can actually get to know.
How does memory work?
▾
Each companion maintains a persistent memory of your conversations — important details you've shared, emotional moments, recurring topics, and how the relationship has evolved. This memory grows over time, allowing the companion to reference past conversations, acknowledge milestones, and become increasingly attuned to who you are. Your memory is yours and can be reviewed or managed at any time.
Is Chatrix free to use?
▾
Chatrix will offer a free tier with core features available to everyone. Premium subscription plans will unlock expanded memory depth, additional companion slots, enhanced personality customization, and priority features. Exact pricing will be announced closer to launch. Early waitlist members will receive special founding-member pricing.
Are my conversations private?
▾
Yes — absolutely. Privacy is foundational to how Chatrix is built, not an afterthought. Your conversations are encrypted, never sold to third parties, and never used to train models without your explicit consent. You own your data. You can export it, review it, or delete it entirely at any time through your account settings.
